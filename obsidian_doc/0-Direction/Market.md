Sourced data for pitch deck, investor conversations, and "why now" arguments. All numbers have sources — don't cite without checking the source is still valid.

## Why Now — The Arc

Three forces converging:

1. **Business model proven in adjacent categories.** Booksy validated SaaS + marketplace for fragmented service providers, from Warsaw to $1.3B GMV. Sports coaching is even more fragmented and operationally harder (groups, cancellations, backfill) — meaning tool lock-in is stronger.
2. **Smart money entering sports coaching.** TeachMe.To raised $10M (Sam Altman), CoachIQ $1.3M pre-seed, Playtomic $110M+. Category is getting funded, but nobody combines tool + marketplace. The gap is wide open.
3. **Expectations shifted.** Coaches and schools now expect mobile-first, lightweight software. And every empty slot hurts more — customer acquisition is expensive, so idle capacity is real money lost.

## Poland — Bulletproof Numbers

### Demand at record levels

- **71% of Polish children** in paid extracurricular sports — record since tracking started in 1998. Families spend PLN 944/month on activities, 57% on sports specifically. (SW Research/Compensa 2025)
- **16,612 sports clubs** in Poland, up 14.8% from 2022. **1.4M people** exercising in clubs, up 30.1%. (GUS 2024)
- **48,100 licensed coaches/instructors** (GUS 2023) — only federation-licensed, real number is multiples higher.

### Booksy proved the exact playbook from Warsaw

- **PLN 407M revenue** (2024), $269M raised, $10B+ GMV. Same model: vertical booking for fragmented service providers. Filed with KRS — legally verified.
- 260M appointments, 140K businesses, 30M+ consumers, 9M bookings/month.
- Revenue nearly doubled: $35.2M (2023) → $65.9M (2024).

### Competition is weak and proves WTP

- **ActiveNow**: 2,000+ schools paying 125–1,680 PLN/month. 9 employees. Dated UX, no marketplace, no mobile app worth using. → Your lead list.

### Padel exploding

- **30 courts (2021) → ~270 (early 2025) → 500 projected by year-end.** 90K amateur players. New coaches entering without entrenched WhatsApp habits.

### Fitness penetration gap

- Poland at **8% gym penetration** vs 10–15% EU average. Growth ceiling hasn't been hit — millions of new members coming.

- No public PZT registered player count

## Global / US Data
