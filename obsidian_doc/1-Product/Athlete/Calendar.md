# Athlete — Calendar

Your schedule, all in one place.

## Layout

- Weekly view, horizontal split: today's trainings on top, upcoming days scrollable below
- Each training shows: time, group name (human-readable), venue, coach

## Training detail (tap into training)

- Location with map link
- Time and duration
- Coach name
- Other athletes in the group
- Training notes (if coach added any)
- Group chat (read and send messages in context)

## History

- Past trainings visible below the current week
- Collapsible
