# Athlete — Home

The only page most athletes open daily. Answers: "Do I need to confirm anything?"

## Sections

- **Next Training** — confirm/decline card. Shows day + venue + time + coach name + who else confirmed ("Anna, Mark +1"). One tap action.
- **Group messages** — unread messages from coach surface as cards. "Tuesday Tennis: 'Court changed to outdoor #3'". Visible immediately, not buried behind taps.
- **Open Spots** — available trainings from your coaches. Only shown when spots exist to claim. Hidden when empty.
- **Offers** — new group invites, waitlist promotions, spot you've been offered. Action-required items only.

## Interactions

- Tap confirm/decline → done, card updates or disappears
- Tap open spot → claim it (one tap) or view training details
- Tap offer → accept/decline

## Design notes

- If everything is confirmed and nothing is pending, Home should be nearly empty. That's the win state.
- Group messages surface as cards on Home, not just a header badge. A time-sensitive "court changed" message must be visible the moment you open the app — not 3 taps deep.
