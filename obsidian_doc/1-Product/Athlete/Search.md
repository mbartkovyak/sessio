# Athlete — Search

Find new coaches and training opportunities.

## Search & filters

- **Sport** (tennis, padel, etc.)
- **City / area**
- **Rating** (minimum stars)
- **Availability** (has open spots)
- **Training type** (group, individual, or both)

## Results

- **Coach cards**: photo, name, sport, location, star rating (out of 5), review count, bio snippet
- At low volume (launch): show as curated list with category headers, not an empty search bar with zero results

## Coach Profile (tap into card)

- Photo, name, sport(s), location
- Star rating + review count
- Bio / description
- Intro video (if uploaded)
- Athlete reviews (scrollable)
- **Available trainings**: group trainings with open spots + individual training availability
- **"Request to join"** (group) or **"Book individual training"** → coach gets notified, accepts/declines
