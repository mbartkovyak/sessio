# Coach — Home

The command center. Answers: "Do I need to do anything right now?"

## Sections

- **Today's lessons** — list of all lessons (group + individual) with confirmation status (e.g. "3/4 confirmed" or "Confirmed" for individual). Tap to drill in.
- **Alerts** — actionable problems only. Open spot with no waitlist. Athlete hasn't responded with <2h left. Unread messages. Individual lesson unconfirmed.
- **Join requests** — athletes from Search who want in. Accept/decline.
- **This week** — upcoming lessons with fill status at a glance.

## Interactions

- Tap lesson → lesson detail (who's in, manual override)
- Tap alert → goes to the relevant lesson/lesson/chat
- Tap join request → see athlete info, assign to lesson

## Design notes

- If no alerts and everything confirmed, Home should feel calm. That's the goal state.
- Alerts sorted by urgency (time proximity).
- Unread messages from lesson chats surface here — coach doesn't need to hunt through Lessons tab.
