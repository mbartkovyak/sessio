# Coach — Lessons

Where the coach sets up and manages everything they offer. Both group and individual lessons live here.

## Lesson list

- **Search athletes** — search bar at top. Finds athletes across all lessons. "Which lesson is Anna in?"
- All lessons with: name, type (group/individual), schedule, athlete count, venue
- Create new lesson button → choose group or individual

## Group lesson detail

Tabs within a group lesson:

- **Roster** — regular members. Tap athlete → attendance stats, strike history.
- **Schedule** — recurring pattern + upcoming instances
- **Chat** — group message thread. Coach + all members. Replaces WhatsApp group.
- **Invite link** — copy/share
- **Attendance** — per-athlete rates, trends

### Group settings

- Name, sport, max spots, venue
- Schedule (day, time, recurrence)
- Confirmation window: 24h or 48h before
- Booking mode: instant vs approval-required
- Visibility: discoverable in Search or invite-only

## Individual lesson detail

Tabs within an individual lesson:

- **Athlete** — who this lesson is with, their attendance stats
- **Schedule** — recurring pattern or one-off lessons
- **Chat** — direct message thread. Coach ↔ athlete only.
- **Attendance** — history, cancellation rate

### Individual settings

- Name, sport, venue
- Schedule (day, time, recurrence or one-off)
- Confirmation window: 24h or 48h before
- Visibility: discoverable in Search or private

## Chat (both types)

- Simple text messages. Handles: "court changed", "running late", "cancelled due to rain"
- No media, no file sharing, no read receipts. Dead simple.
- Group lesson → group chat. Individual lesson → direct chat.
