# School View — Dashboard

Owner only. Members don't have access to this view. Answers: "How is my school doing this week?"

## Sections

- **Coaches** — list of all coaches with: name, active lessons count, total athletes, fill rate this week. Tap coach → their public profile.
- **This week** — aggregate: total lessons across all coaches, total confirmations pending, open spots with no waitlist.
- **Alerts** — school-level problems only. Coach with multiple lessons at risk. Unusual no-show spike. Venue conflict between two coaches.
- **Invite link** — copy/share to add new coaches.

## Design notes

- Owner cannot modify other coaches' lessons from here — visibility only.
- Alerts are informational. Owner follows up with the coach directly (outside the app for now).
