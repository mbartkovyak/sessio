## Key facts

- Uses **ActiveNow** for everything — scheduling, attendance, cancellations, payments, notifications
- Has been on ActiveNow **for several years** — app has improved over time

## How he coordinates

- **Everything through ActiveNow** — client is added to the app, then self-manages attendance/cancellations within coach-defined rules
- **Cancellation policy:** 3 hours before lesson = can reschedule; after that = lesson lost (with exceptions for loyal long-term clients)
- **Instructors have their own logins** with limited access (he's not sure exactly what they can see)
- **SMS notifications** through the app — free monthly quota, can buy more, rarely needs to
- **Payment tracking** — app shows who has outstanding payments and auto-reminds
- **Substitution notifications** — app tells parents when a different instructor covers a class

## ActiveNow experience

- **Onboarding took 1-2 seasons** — not everyone adopted immediately, some parents still messaged him directly
- Now **~80% of clients actively use** the app — "nie męczą mnie prywatnymi wiadomościami"
- **Setup work is front-loaded** — big effort in September planning groups/schedule, then it runs itself
- **Pricing:** tiered by number of active users, can pause anytime, small transaction fee on payments
- Overall very positive: "naprawdę polecane"

## Surprising insights

1. **ActiveNow is the dominant tool in Polish swimming schools.** This is now the second reference (also mentioned in ActiveNow Analysis). Coaches actively recommend it to others.
2. **1-2 season adoption curve.** Even with a good tool, it takes time to get parents on board — 80% after multiple seasons. Sessio should expect similar adoption friction.
3. **Free trial lesson BEFORE adding to system.** He doesn't onboard someone until they commit — the app is for managing existing clients, not acquiring new ones.
4. **He basically advertised ActiveNow unprompted.** Strong product loyalty — hard to switch someone like this.

## Key takeaway for Sessio

**Confirms ActiveNow's dominance in PL swimming.** This coach is happy, locked in, and evangelizing the product. Competing head-on with ActiveNow in swimming is tough — their feature set (payments, SMS, attendance, make-ups) is mature. Sessio's angle needs to be different: discovery/marketplace, or target sports where ActiveNow has less penetration.
