## Surprising insights

1. **An "ActiveNow is fine" coach is still shopping.** Even when the core product works, pricing + analytics + API gaps push school owners to look around. "Good enough" is not a moat at this scale.
2. **Polish school owners want AI integration.** Bartosz proactively wanted to plug ActiveNow into an AI agent — the missing API is what blocked him. He went further and named the direction: smart proposals, background analytics, suggestions — not dashboards. Strong signal that AI-readiness is becoming a buying criterion at scale.
3. **Reporting/analytics is the gap, not scheduling.** At ~1000 clients, the daily ops are handled — what's missing is the layer above: custom reports, pivots, data export. Different pain than the manual-Excel coaches (Witek).
4. **Pricing sensitivity even at scale.** 2 schools / 1000 clients should be the segment that pays — and even he is shopping for cheaper.

## Key takeaway for Sessio

**Different segment, different pain.** Bartosz isn't a Sessio target right now — ActiveNow already solves his core scheduling problem. But the call validates three things:
- **API + AI-ready** is a real selling point at the school-owner tier. If Sessio ships an open API and clean data export early, it differentiates from the incumbent.
- **Analytics layer matters** once you cross ~500–1000 clients. Custom reports / pivots / data export should be on the roadmap for the school product, not an afterthought.
- **AI is the wedge.** A satisfied incumbent customer told us unprompted that the way forward is smart proposals + background analytics + suggestions. That's a positioning gift — Sessio can lead with "AI-native school OS" while ActiveNow plays catch-up with reports.

Not a near-term lead, but a useful data point on what the incumbent is missing.
