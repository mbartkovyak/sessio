## Key facts

- Was near full-time (6 days/week), now transitioning to another industry
- Colleague still active as swimming coach
- Mostly **individual** lessons, some groups
- Fully independent — no club/school. Finds clients through **Instagram/Facebook ads** (geo-targeted) and **Google business card**
- Over time, referrals took over — at peak didn't need ads anymore, people were waiting for slots
## How he coordinates

- **SMS the day before** — "are we on tomorrow?" to confirm
- **24h cancellation policy** — cancel late = pay full price
- People forget, so confirmation is essential

## Tools used

- **ActiveNow** (~100 PLN/month, pricing scales with client count)
- Stopped using it — felt he could manage without it, and the app "wasn't great"
- ActiveNow pros: SMS gateway for bulk messages, clients could mark absence and pick makeup group sessions
- ActiveNow cons: **no push notification when a client cancels** — had to manually check every day. Dealbreaker.

## Biggest pain: gaps in schedule

**"Skaranie boskie"** (absolute nightmare) — when a client cancels an individual lesson, he's stuck sitting at the pool for 45 min doing nothing. Wants the app to **automatically message other clients**: "can you come 30 min earlier?" to close gaps. This was his #1 ask, repeated multiple times.

## Reactions to product concepts

| Concept | Reaction |
|---------|----------|
| Auto-confirm + waitlist backfill for groups | "Would be nice" — sees it as useful, especially for groups |
| Churn follow-up (auto-remind inactive clients) | Admitted he didn't do it manually but **would use it if automated** — "trzeba walczyć o tych klientów" (you have to fight for your clients) |
| Availability-based booking (clients see open slots, send request) | **Very excited** — directly solves the gap problem. "Szczególnie w zajęciach indywidualnych" |
| Auto-reschedule on cancellation | **#1 want.** Repeated it as final comment. The gap-filling feature. |
| Two-sided app (coach view + client view) | Strong yes — "it should work from both sides." ActiveNow had this but poorly. |
| Payments in app | Prefers cash/BLIK. ActiveNow had HotPay (2% fee) — he avoided it. Not a buy-in feature. |

## Willingness to pay

- **100-200 PLN/month** if full-time coaching
- Acknowledges it depends on volume — more clients/groups = more value
- ActiveNow was ~100 PLN/month and he paid it

## Competition intel: ActiveNow

- Popular among swimming schools in Poland
- Clients use it too (two-sided)
- Has group makeup session feature (pick another group to attend)
- SMS gateway for bulk comms
- Used it to send holiday wishes to all clients — liked that touch
- **Many coaches not satisfied** — colleague says "duża część szukałaby czegoś innego" (many would look for alternatives)
- They "regularly develop it" (email bombardment) but core UX issues remain

## Surprising insights

1. **Auto-reschedule > auto-confirm.** For individual-heavy coaches, filling gaps matters more than group waitlists
2. **Notifications are table stakes.** ActiveNow lost him partly because it didn't push-notify on cancellations
3. **Referrals solved his acquisition** — discovery/leads feature is less urgent for established coaches
4. **Payments = not a buy-in.** Coaches prefer cash/BLIK, don't want a % fee
5. **Holiday wishes via SMS** — small CRM touch that coaches value
