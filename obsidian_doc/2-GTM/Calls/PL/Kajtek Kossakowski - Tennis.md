## Key facts

- Runs a tennis academy — **Akademia Warszawska dla kobiet** (women's tennis academy in Warsaw), expanding to Mińsk
- **~120-130 people** trained weekly — all groups, zero individual lessons
- **15-20% absence rate** in group lessons — this is the scale of the backfill/makeup problem he manages manually
- **5 trainers** — they only coach, Kajtek handles all admin/scheduling/attendance
- Never tried any scheduling tool — manages everything via **Excel + WhatsApp**
- Payments via **bank transfer** (standing orders), upfront monthly — clients have auto-transfers set up
- Plans to **scale across Poland** — different from typical coaches/academies. Operates across multiple venues, not tied to own courts
- Has a website for the Warsaw academy, not yet for Mińsk

## How he coordinates

- Each group has its own **WhatsApp group** (small, ~4 people)
- **Wednesday:** asks on group who will come to Friday training
- **Thursday:** proposes makeup sessions based on known absences
- **Monthly:** asks on main group who has absences to make up, tracks in Excel manually
- **24h cancellation policy** — cancel late = lesson counted but can still be made up (he's flexible). No-show without notice = lesson lost unless he finds a replacement

## Tools used

- **None.** Pure Excel + WhatsApp
- Never tried any app — "ja się na tym nie znam" (I don't know about these things)
- Has no awareness of ActiveNow or similar tools

## Biggest pain: scale

At current scale (~120 people, ~5 trainers), everything is **"worked out"** — systems are manual but functional. Pain is **future-facing:**
- Can't write to 100 WhatsApp groups manually
- Can't track attendance in Excel for hundreds of people
- Wants auto-attendance (people mark presence themselves) and auto-notifications

## Reactions to product concepts

| Concept | Reaction |
|---------|----------|
| Auto-confirm + attendance tracking | **Interested** — biggest value is removing manual Excel tracking and WhatsApp messaging at scale |
| Public profile on Sessio platform | **Yes** — "ja bym oczekiwał, żeby to mieć też na twojej platformie" (I'd expect to have it on your platform too) |
| Churn follow-up (auto-remind inactive) | **Useful for kids** — parents disappear without notice, blocks spots. Adults have closer relationship + contracts, less relevant |
| WhatsApp bot for confirmation | **50-50** — some users struggle even with WhatsApp. Older people may not engage |
| Payments in app | **No** — clients have standing bank transfers, it works fine |
| Individual lessons | **Not doing them** — groups only, building community. Individuals "don't pay off" at this stage |

## Willingness to pay

- "Musiałbym zobaczyć" (I'd need to see it) — wants a **trial period**
- Would pay **a few hundred PLN/month** if the app truly eliminates manual work
- **Not 1000 PLN/month** at current scale
- Key concern: will older users actually adopt it? If they don't, he's paying for the app AND still doing manual work

## Critical insight: ONE channel, no split

**Strongest opinion in the call.** Kajtek previously had chaos with mixed Messenger/Instagram/SMS/WhatsApp — people got lost, he forgot where to check, errors happened (6 people showing up for a 4-person court). Fixed it by forcing everyone onto WhatsApp.

If Sessio is adopted, he wants **everyone on the app** — no parallel WhatsApp groups. "Nie możemy dla jednej grupy mieć na WhatsAppie komunikatora i jeszcze na tej apce — bo to bez sensu, bałagan się zrobi." (We can't have WhatsApp for one group and the app for another — it makes no sense, it'll be a mess.)

Implication: the WhatsApp fallback idea is rejected. If Sessio is the tool, it must be the **only** channel. This is a high adoption bar but also a strong lock-in signal.

## Surprising insights

1. **Pain is future, not present.** At ~130 people the manual system works. The buy-in is "I'm scaling to all of Poland and I'll need this." Not an urgent burning problem today.
2. **School ≠ coach.** Kajtek's model (owner managing 5+ trainers, trainers don't touch admin) is different from solo coach. School admin view matters.
3. **One channel or nothing.** No hybrid WhatsApp + app. He'll either migrate everyone or not adopt at all. This raises the adoption bar but means stronger retention once in.
4. **Contracts exist.** Adults sign contracts with cancellation via email — churn follow-up is less relevant for adults, more for kids whose parents just stop showing up.
5. **Trainers are excluded from admin.** They don't need the app for scheduling — only Kajtek (the owner) does. Trainer-facing features are low priority for this persona.
