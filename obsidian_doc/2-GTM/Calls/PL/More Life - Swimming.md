## Key facts

- Swimming school operating across **10-12 pools in Warsaw** — significant scale
- **No scheduling/management software** — deliberate choice
- Each pool has a **coordinator** (also an instructor) who manages attendance, cancellations, communication
- Coordination via **SMS + WhatsApp groups** — one per pool
- Frequently **moves kids between pools** (small ↔ large) and **reshuffles groups** based on skill progress
- Decided automated systems **don't fit their model** — discussed it internally and rejected the idea

## How they coordinate

- **Coordinator per pool** — an instructor who works there 2-3x/week, knows the kids and parents
- Parents SMS the coordinator to cancel or ask about make-up times
- Coordinator maintains **attendance lists** and checks skill levels to place kids in correct groups
- They **don't give parents free choice** of when to come — coordinator controls placement to ensure level-appropriate groups
- WhatsApp groups for internal team communication

## Why they rejected software

- Too much **group mixing and pool migration** — kids change groups as they progress, move between pools
- They want a **human on the other end** ("jest po drugiej stronie telefonu żywy człowiek, a nie automat")
- Concern that self-service systems let parents self-declare attendance without instructor oversight
- One person couldn't handle the scale → decentralized to coordinators instead of centralizing in software

## Surprising insights

1. **Scale without software is possible** — 10-12 pools in Warsaw, no app, just coordinators + SMS/WhatsApp. Shows that human coordination scales if you hire/delegate.
2. **Objection to automation: loss of control.** Their concern is that apps let parents self-schedule without instructor knowing group composition — quality control argument.
3. **Offered to ask colleagues** what system they use — knows other schools DO use management apps. Suggested sending an SMS for follow-up.

## Key takeaway for Sessio

**Not a prospect.** Deliberately chose human coordination over software. Their model works because they have coordinators at each pool. The loss-of-control objection ("parent decides, instructor doesn't know who's coming") is worth noting — Sessio should ensure coaches always have visibility and control over who attends.
