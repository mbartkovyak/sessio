## How he coordinates

- Color-codes clients in his system: **regular clients** vs **one-off clients**
- Runs a **Telegram group** with all clients — multiple chats:
  - General announcements (e.g. "I'm sick today" — everyone sees it, no individual messages)
  - **Cancellations chat** — clients write there regardless of whether payment burns or not, so Anton always has visibility
- When a slot opens up, he manually reaches out to potential clients he knows might want it

## Cancellation policy

- **24-hour rule** — more of a formality, slightly earlier/later is fine
- But cancelling at 8-9pm for a 10am session next day = 100% not a valid cancellation, payment burns
- Rare late cancellations — usually genuine force majeure, handled case-by-case
- **Single late cancel** from a regular client → full refund, no questions. Only flags if systematic

## Group lessons (kids)

- **Monthly subscription** — fixed court rental fee paid regardless of attendance
- If kid misses: their **court share burns**, but training fee carries over to next month
- "It's a group, you can't adjust for one kid because of grandma's birthday"
- Acknowledged this is hard to systematize in an app — needs flexibility for coach to manually override

## Club app (Atmosphera) — what doesn't work

| Problem | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Only hour blocks** | His sessions vary: 1h, 1.5h, 2.5h. App only allows full-hour slots — can't represent real schedule |
| **Payment routing broken** | If coach books the court → client can't pay through app. If client books → can pay via Apple Pay/Google Pay. Backwards |
| **Promised features missing** | "They promised a lot, not even close to delivering" |

## His ideal app

**Deposit-based system** where:
1. Each client has a **deposit account** in the app
2. Coach sets schedule in advance
3. On booking confirmation, **funds auto-deduct** from client's balance
4. App acts as **guarantor** — holds money until training completes
5. If cancelled on time → refund to balance. If completed → splits to club + coach accounts
6. No manual payment chasing

## Client acquisition

- One Instagram video got massive response — **niche is empty in Zhytomyr**, nobody does tennis content there
- Gets surfaced first to anyone searching for tennis in Zhytomyr
- Currently **demand exceeds capacity** — "I have nowhere to put new people"
- Wants to develop Instagram as personal brand but has no time

## Surprising insights

1. **Demand > supply.** He's turning people away. The problem isn't finding clients — it's finding time. Backfill is useful not for revenue recovery but for **convenience** (not having to manually coordinate).
2. **The Telegram cancellation chat is a workaround for a missing product.** He built a structured communication system (announcement chat, cancellation chat) because no app does it. Sessio's messaging-per-lesson model maps directly to this.
3. **Club apps disappoint.** Atmosphera promised a lot and delivered little. Coaches are frustrated with tools built for clubs, not for coaches. There's an opening for coach-first tools.
4. **Deposit/guarantor model is his dream.** He independently described an escrow-style payment system. This validates future payment features — coaches want the app to handle money, not just scheduling.
5. **Cancellation rate: ~10-20%** of trainings per week. Varies cyclically (summer, school breaks = more cancellations). Not catastrophic but consistent enough to matter.

## Next step

- Open to testing when product is more mature
- Good candidate: full-time, high volume, already structured his own systems, would adopt a better tool
- Follow up when payment features are on the roadmap — that's his #1 desire
