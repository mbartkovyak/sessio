## Biggest pain: payment visibility

The core problem is **delayed visibility into payment status:**

- Rule: first session is a free trial, after that you must pay upfront
- Coaches sometimes let kids train on credit (against the rules) due to "character or circumstances"
- Ruslan only discovers debts at month-end — "the ship has already sailed"
- People occasionally train for a month, then disappear without paying
- Rare but happens — at 2000 members, even rare = real money

**His words:** "If I had an app where I could see payment status per person per venue throughout the month, I could intervene early instead of finding out at month-end when it's too late."

## Surprising insights

1. **Scale amplifies manual process failures.** At 2000 members across 30 coaches, even small rule violations (training on credit) become systemic. He can't monitor 30 coaches daily.
2. **Decentralized = no admin.** No central venue means no receptionist option. Everything falls on coaches who should be coaching, not collecting money.
3. **The joke tells the truth.** "I need to microchip the coaches to see where they are and what they're thinking" — real pain is lack of coach oversight, not athlete management.
4. **He didn't ask about features.** He asked about visibility and control. This is a school-owner persona, not a coach persona.
