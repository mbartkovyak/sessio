## Key facts

- Works as a trainer in a **Sportlife club network** — Zhytomyr branch, first in their network to use remote registration via app
- Previously everything was analog: clients came to reception, asked for a specific trainer, receptionist signed them up manually
- Now moved to an **in-house app** — clients can sign up from home, 24h before training
- He handles kids group sessions — typically 5 kids registered, sometimes only 3 show up
- He manually marks attendance in the app — if a kid doesn't show, he doesn't mark them, so their session doesn't burn

---

## What works in their current app

- **Remote registration** — parents can sign up from home 24h before, convenient
- Clients are generally satisfied with the booking flow
- Moved from fully analog (reception desk) to digital — big improvement

## What doesn't work

| Problem | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Can't cancel bookings** | Clients can sign up but can't unsubscribe themselves. Creates anxiety — "will my session burn if I can't come?" Evgeniy works around it by not marking absent kids, but new clients don't know this and stress about it |
| **Wrong trainer bookings** | Multiple trainers run sessions back-to-back. Parents sometimes sign up for the wrong trainer by mistake. Staff then has to: remove bracelet, manually unregister, re-register — can't do it in the app because child is already booked with another trainer |
| **No expiry notifications** | For pool access: subscription + medical certificate required. No push notification when subscription is about to expire. Kids show up, can't enter the pool, get confused ("why does it work for others but not me?"). Creates conflict situations |
| **No medical certificate tracking** | Club subscription works but pool certificate expired = can enter club but not pool. Another source of confusion and confrontation |

## His suggestions

1. **Self-service cancel/reschedule** — let clients unsubscribe themselves so trainers can see real attendance numbers in advance and plan accordingly
2. **Expiry reminders** — push notification 3 days before + day-of that subscription/certificate is expiring
3. **Prevent wrong-trainer bookings** — if a client has an agreement with a specific trainer, block registration with random other trainers

## Surprising insights

1. **He's an employee, not an owner.** Different persona — he experiences the app's pain as a frontline user, not as a decision-maker. His feedback is operational, not strategic.
2. **The "no cancel" workaround is trust-based.** He manually doesn't mark absent kids to save their sessions — but this only works because he told clients directly. New clients don't know and panic. Systemic fix needed.
3. **Analog → digital transition is recent.** Their Zhytomyr branch is the first in the Sportlife network to go digital. Other branches still use reception desk + manual registration. Shows how early this market is.
4. **Bracelet/physical access system** adds complexity — the app must integrate with physical access control (pool entry), not just scheduling.

## Next step

- Useful as a frontline-user perspective on what breaks in existing scheduling apps
