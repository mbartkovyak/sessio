2. **The buyer is the facility/school, not the coach.** For coaches like Doug, Sessio would need to sell to the tennis facility, not the individual coach.
3. **Waitlist is manual but functional.** Front desk calls waitlist people, or the canceling player finds their own replacement. Works at this scale.

## Key takeaway for Sessio
