# Go-to-Market

> **Pitch reframing (after Maxime Pioneers debrief 2026-04-15):** lead with "fill empty spots," not "solve coach admin." Admin was solvable 20 years ago; the differentiated unlock is connecting scheduling to a marketplace that fills underutilized inventory. See `Maxime Pioneers - last session feedback.md` before updating the deck.

## Beachhead: Warsaw

Why Warsaw:
- Personal network with coaches (tennis, swimming already in conversation).
- ActiveNow (Warsaw-based) proves the market pays — but coaches are unhappy.
- Booksy already trained the market on "find → book" behavior.
- No dominant coaching-specific platform.

The product is sport-agnostic — scheduling, confirmations, slot-filling work the same for any sport. Warsaw is the constraint, not the sport.

## Launch sequence

1. **Validate** — discovery calls with coaches across sports. Understand their workflow, confirm the pain, test product concepts. → `Calls/` for notes.
2. **Seed supply** — onboard 5-10 coaches in Warsaw. Personal network first, then cold outreach (Instagram DMs, Google Maps, ActiveNow coach directory).
3. **Coaches bring demand** — coach shares link with existing clients → clients join → marketplace starts filling organically. Each coach is a distribution channel.
4. **Build density** — more coaches, more sports, same city. Athletes start discovering coaches through the platform instead of Instagram ads.
5. **Expand cities** — only after Warsaw proves the model works.

Recurring insights from the calls:
- Gap-filling ("skaranie boskie") is the repeated #1 ask. Czarek repeated it, Kajtek confirmed it, every swim school said the same.
- ActiveNow unhappiness validates WTP — people pay 100+ PLN/month for software that doesn't send cancel notifications.
- Schools (not just solo coaches) want the tool. One school onboarded = 5-20 coaches with marketplace profiles + hundreds of athletes overnight.

## Key metrics

1. **Adoption** — does the coach use Sessio as their main scheduling tool?
2. **Athlete experience** — are athletes confirming/booking through Sessio without friction?
3. **Gap-filling** — when a slot opens up, is it actually getting filled by the system?
4. **Discovery** — is the coach's profile generating new athlete interest? (matters later, once density exists)

Everything else is secondary right now.
