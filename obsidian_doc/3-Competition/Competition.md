# Competition

No one combines scheduling tool + marketplace for independent coaches. That's the gap.

**Primary competitor right now: TeachMe.To** — closest model to what Sessio's marketplace could become, and already expanding into Europe (France). Key difference: they're a pure marketplace with zero tool value for coaches.

## Dead marketplaces — the lesson

**CoachUp** (US, $10M+ raised, stalling) and **HOKALI** (YC-backed, pivoted away) both tried marketplace-first and failed. You can't cold-start a coaching marketplace without tool value.

Sessio avoids this: tool-first → coaches bring their own athletes → marketplace emerges organically.

---

## Pricing benchmarks

- Coach tools: $10-50/month (CoachNow PRO: $50, CoachIQ: $49-199)
- Studio/school tools: from €9/month usage-based (Fitssey) to 125-1,680 PLN/month by participant count (ActiveNow, free tier available but crippled — no payments, no SMS, no reports)
- Marketplace take rates: ~$0.20/booking (Playtomic) or 10-20% commission

### Patterns

- **Swimming is the dominant segment** — most confirmed customers are swimming schools. Makes sense: groups, schedules, make-up lessons, multi-pool logistics.
- **Tennis schools use it too** — relevant for Sessio's beachhead. These are exactly the kind of schools Sessio could win.
- **Geographic spread** — Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, Katowice, Gdańsk, Kielce, Kołobrzeg. National reach but concentrated in major cities.
- **Typical customer** — 200-600 participants, 5-20 instructors. Mid-size extracurricular schools, not individual coaches.
- **Language schools are a big chunk** — ActiveNow isn't sport-specific, it's "extracurricular school" software. Sessio is sharper: sport coaches only.

### ActiveNow customers = Sessio's lead list

ActiveNow customers are both schools and coaches Sessio should target. Switching costs are real (data migration, staff retraining, parents with accounts, mid-season risk) — but ActiveNow's product is dated and the bar is low.

**Why schools need Sessio too (maybe more than solo coaches):**
- Schools already pay 125-1,680 PLN/month for ActiveNow. Proven willingness to pay.
- Schools have bigger operational pain — 500 kids, 15 instructors, make-ups, billing. More complexity = more value from modern tooling.
- One school onboarded = 10 coaches with marketplace profiles + 500 athletes on the platform overnight.

**What Sessio offers that ActiveNow can't:**
- Each coach inside the school gets their own profile, reputation, and discovery — not just an "instructor" row. ActiveNow structurally can't do this because they don't model coaches as entities.
- The school gets a modern management layer + marketplace/discovery bringing in new athletes. ActiveNow is a closed admin tool with zero lead generation.
- Athletes get a real app experience — not a WordPress widget with no input validation.

**Sessio serves both layers:** the school gets operations, the coach gets identity and leads. That's the structural advantage over ActiveNow.

These schools' coaches already understand digital scheduling tools (no education barrier) and have active client bases. The customer database above is a sourcing list for both school-level and coach-level outreach.
