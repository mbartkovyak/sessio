# 4. Monetization — Booksy model

SaaS subscription (school pays monthly) + commission **only on NEW platform-acquired clients**. Existing coach-client relationships are never touched — no disintermediation pressure, no reason for the coach to leave. See `3-Competition/Competition.md` for the ActiveNow pricing table that substantiates willingness-to-pay.

| Revenue line | Sessio |
|---|---|
| **SaaS subscription** (core) | School pays monthly for scheduling, confirmations, dashboard |
| **Commission on NEW clients only** | Happy hour / discovery / marketplace bookings only |
| **Payment processing** | Later, once payments are in-product |
| **Consumer side** | Free |

---

## Phase 1 — Free (now)

Free for beachhead coaches + their school. Goal is daily adoption, not revenue. Coaches keep collecting money however they do today (cash, transfer).

## Phase 2 — SaaS subscription (core, near-term)

School pays monthly for the tool: scheduling, confirmations, auto-backfill, cross-coach dashboard, chat, attendance, passes. Solo coaches pay a lower per-seat equivalent.

Benchmark: ActiveNow charges 125–1,680 PLN/month by participant count, plus SMS add-ons. 2,100+ Polish schools pay it. The willingness is proven — Sessio just has to be better.

## Phase 3 — Commission on NEW platform-acquired clients only

When an athlete discovers a coach via Sessio (search, happy hours, marketplace surfaces) → Sessio takes a cut on that booking. Clients the coach brought themselves via invite link **never carry a commission**. This is the explicit no-disintermediation contract.

Contrast with TeachMe.To (pure marketplace): after one session, coach and athlete move off-platform to WhatsApp, and the platform loses the relationship. Sessio doesn't have this problem because the school is already paying for the tool — the commission is pure upside on leads Sessio generated.

## Phase 4 — Payments + consumer service fee (later)

Once brand trust is established and in-product payments are built, add a small service fee on the consumer side (Airbnb / Doctolib pattern). Trust-dependent, not an early-stage bet.

---

## Why this order

- Zero friction to build supply (free beachhead).
- SaaS covers costs early and proves real daily usage beats marketplace vanity metrics.
- NEW-client-only commission removes the biggest marketplace failure mode (coaches leaving after session one to save the fee).
- Payments arrive last, when the habit is baked in.

## Anti-disintermediation moats

- Recurring auto-scheduling (switching cost is real)
- Cancellation / rescheduling policy enforcement
- Payment processing (coaches hate chasing invoices)
- Review / reputation accumulation (can't take it off-platform)
- Training history data trail
- School-level cross-coach dashboard (only valuable inside Sessio)
