# 4. Monetization

## Phase 1: Free (now)
Free for everyone. Goal is adoption, not revenue. Coaches keep collecting money however they do now (cash, transfer).

## Phase 2: Coach-side commission (after validation)
- Free to list and join
- ~25-30% on first training with a new client (platform brought the client)
- ~10-15% on recurring trainings
- Decreasing commission as coach accumulates hours (Preply model: 33% → 18%)

## Phase 3: Add client-side service fee
- Small service fee on client side (like Airbnb)
- Only after brand trust is established

## Why this order
- Zero friction to build supply (free)
- Commission model proven by Preply ($1.2B, EBITDA positive) and Booksy
- Front-loads platform value where it matters most: discovery and first match
- Becomes cheaper for loyal coaches → retention incentive

## Anti-disintermediation moats
- Recurring auto-scheduling (switching cost is real)
- Cancellation/rescheduling policy enforcement
- Payment processing (coaches hate chasing invoices)
- Review/reputation accumulation (can't take it off-platform)
- Training history data trail
