## Product

### "Rent a coach" — paid discovery calls
Coach ignores your message? Book their time as a training slot, but use it for a 30-min discovery conversation instead. You're essentially paying for their attention — the coach gets compensated at their normal rate, you get the meeting.

**Why it's interesting:** Coaches are busy and ignore cold outreach. Framing a sales/partnership conversation as a paid booking removes friction — they show up because it's money. Works for B2B outreach (Sessio pitching coaches), partnerships, or even athletes vetting a new coach before committing.

**Open questions:** Does the coach know upfront it's a "talk" not a training? Or does it just look like a normal booking? Could this be a standard Sessio feature — "book a consultation" slot type?

## Growth

### Referral discounts that force adoption
Coaches get a discount on their subscription for every client they bring into the app. Refer another coach → even bigger discount.

**Why this works:** Solves the "download, don't use, delete" problem on freemium. The coach has a financial incentive to not just sign up, but actually onboard their clients. And once clients are on the platform, they discover other coaches — which grows the marketplace side organically.

**Mechanics:**
- Coach invites clients → gets X% off per active client
- Coach refers another coach → gets bigger discount (or free month)
- Clients are now on the platform → they see other coaches → they book with others too
- Coach adoption is real (they brought people in), not just a registration

**Why it's better than pure freemium:** Freemium lets coaches sit passively. This makes the discount conditional on action — bringing clients in. The coach becomes the distribution channel, and every onboarded client feeds the marketplace.

## Retention / CRM

### Churn tracking with tags
Follow athletes who drop off — tag them by reason (seasonal, injury, switched coach, price, ghosted). Build a lightweight CRM view in the coach dashboard so coaches can:
- See who hasn't booked in X weeks
- Filter by tag
- Send a re-engagement message (template or custom)

**Why it matters:** Coaches lose clients silently. A nudge at the right time ("haven't seen you in 3 weeks — want to book?") can recover revenue with zero acquisition cost.

**MVP version:** Simple "inactive athletes" list with last-activity date. Tags and messaging come later.
